We invite the submission of research papers, position papers, and demo proposals on the topic of world models for robot learning. This workshop focuses on the intersection of world models and robotics, exploring how predictive models of environment dynamics can advance Physical AI.

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

* **World Action Models (WAMs):** Unifying world dynamics, spatiotemporal understanding, and robot action generation.
* **Learning, reasoning, and evaluation with imagined rollouts:** Leveraging latent/observation space rollouts for planning, causal reasoning, and safety.
* **Multi-modality beyond vision:** Integrating tactile sensing, proprioception, force feedback, audio, and other modalities.
* **Physical accuracy and spatiotemporal consistency:** Simulating stable reality, realistic dynamics, and reliable control.
* **Evaluation metrics and benchmarks:** Action-conditioned metrics and standardized benchmarks focusing on physical plausibility.
